04 / Assumptions you can inspect

Know what the sliders mean.

Origin ≠ nationality

We link an origin to the identically named hotel nationality. That is a provisional bridge, not measured passenger routing. Indirect travel, other airports and residents can weaken the relationship. Twelve nationalities without a direct origin use a seasonal forecast and have no aviation controls.

Prediction is conditional, not a causal estimate

Calendar and trend controls reduce some confounding. They do not prove that adding flights creates the predicted demand. Historical validation uses observed aviation inputs. A changed flight plan is a scenario, not a guarantee.

Guest-days, visitors and room occupancy

The target is the supplied daily Guests count. Monthly sums are reported guest-days. Visitor capture and length of stay are not separately identified here. No room inventory was supplied, so this prototype does not calculate occupancy or claim true guest nights.

Load factor and timing

Baseline load factor excludes infants where complete, with a fixed infant count adjustment to recover total passengers. Ratios above 100% remain visible as source-data exceptions. Passenger exposure is averaged over seven days, or uses the selected stay kernel. This is an empirical timing feature, not a measured stay distribution.

Scope of this version

Existing origin markets only; unsupported new routes need an explicit bridge and a prior. Domestic demand is excluded. Estimated event dates, future hotel arrivals, occupancy and manually assumed capture probabilities are not predictors. Future supplied periods have no observed Guests labels.
